# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Timestral Forecast panel**: any time series plus a forecast with 50–98% confidence bands or a
  quantile fan; related queries and calendar features as inputs; threshold ETA; backtesting with
  anomaly markers.
- **Forecast with Timestral** in panel menus and Explore, with *Add to dashboard*.
- **Timestral data source**: forecasts of any data source's queries for every visualization and for
  alert rules, including the time until a threshold is crossed.
- Includes Mistral AI's Timestral-209M model; no external services needed.
